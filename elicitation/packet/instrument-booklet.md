# Rater booklet: judgments about structural-inspection work

*Draft for ethics review. Task texts and their risk and criticality figures in
`items.csv` are drafts and must be set or confirmed by a domain expert before
use. The anchors below are those fixed in App. S4 (`app:elicit-instrument`).*

You are asked for your professional judgment. There are no right answers, and
no individual rating is shown to anyone outside the research team. Please read
`participant-information-sheet.md` first. All ratings use a scale from 0 to 10.

## Part A. What a task teaches (`learn`)

For each of the 12 task descriptions, rate how much *judgment transferable to
other work in this domain* a developing practitioner acquires by doing the task.

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | acquires no judgment transferable to any other task in the domain |
| 5 | acquires judgment transferable within the same task class only |
| 10 | acquires judgment transferable across the domain and required for independent competence |

## Part B. Who still exercises the judgment (`phi`)

For each of the 12 tasks, and for each of three ways of doing it, rate what
share of the judgment the task requires is still exercised by the human.

| Mode | Description for this survey |
|---|---|
| `H+A` | the human is assisted by an AI agent (for example, drafting findings from inspection data) |
| `H+R` | the human is assisted by a robot or remote platform (for example, a crawler or drone collecting the data) |
| `H+A+R` | both |

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | the human observes an outcome and cannot alter it |
| 5 | the human sets the parameters of a machine determination and reviews its output against independent evidence |
| 10 | the human forms the determination and the machine supplies data only |

## Part C. Supervision capacity (`psi_ratio`)

For each of the 4 task classes: what is the **maximum number of developing
practitioners one competent lead can supervise on this class of task** without
the lead's own attention to the task being compromised? Give a number (it may
be a fraction; 0 means a lead cannot supervise anyone on it).

## Part D. Certification hours (`h_raw`)

For the certification route to independent competence in structural inspection:

1. How many supervised clock hours does the **documented** route require?
2. How many supervised clock hours are **actually required in practice**, in
   your judgment?

## Round two

After round one you will see an anonymised summary of everyone's ratings, and
the written reasons attached to the highest and lowest ratings. You may revise
any rating, or keep it. Revising is not expected.
