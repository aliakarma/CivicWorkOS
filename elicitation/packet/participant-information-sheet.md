# Participant information sheet

*Draft. Translate to Arabic and adapt to the committee's template before use.*

**Study.** Professional judgments about developing skill in municipal structural
inspection. **Researcher.** [NAME, institution, contact].

**What this is about.** Cities are beginning to assign inspection work to AI
tools and robots. We are studying how that changes the chance for newer
inspectors to practise the judgment that makes someone fully competent. We want
your professional view on four questions: how much different tasks teach, how
much judgment the inspector keeps when a machine helps, how many trainees one
senior inspector can supervise, and how many supervised hours it really takes
to become independent.

**What you would do.** Two online sessions of about 45 minutes, [two weeks]
apart. In the first you rate items on a 0 to 10 scale. In the second you see an
anonymous summary of the group's ratings and may change yours or keep them.

**It is voluntary.** You may decline, skip any item, or withdraw until [date]
without giving a reason. **Declining or withdrawing has no effect on your job,
your training, or the work you are given, now or under any system a city adopts.**
The research team has no role in allocating work and will not be told who was
asked.

**Who sees your answers.** You are given a code name. Your name is never
attached to your ratings. Only the research team sees individual ratings.
Nobody, including your employer, will be told whether you took part or what you
rated. Results are reported for groups only, and never for a group of fewer
than four people. Written reasons you give are screened so they cannot identify
you or a structure before anyone else sees them.

**What you gain and what it costs.** There is no payment [confirm]. The risk is
small: some people feel uneasy rating how trainees learn. You may stop at any
time.

**What happens to the data.** Stored at [location] for [period], then destroyed.
De-identified ratings may be published with the article so others can check the
analysis.

**Questions or concerns.** [Researcher contact]. Concerns about the conduct of
the study: [committee contact and approval reference].
