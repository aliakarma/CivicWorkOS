Dear Editors,

We submit the manuscript *CivicWorkOS: Capability-Preserving Allocation of Municipal Work Among Humans, AI Agents and Robots* for consideration in Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence as a **Hypothesis and Theory** article. The main text is 11,491 words, with 4 figures and 10 tables; a Supplementary Material document carries the proofs, the full contestability procedure, the extended related work and the specified evaluation protocol.

The article proposes that municipal allocation of work among human staff, AI agents and robots be treated as a governed decision over *staffed modes*, which pair an execution mode with a named roster at declared career stages. It prices the developmental practice that automation removes through a Human Capability Preservation Budget, and it gives the city-wide program, an online Lagrangian rule, a feasibility condition, and an intake requirement for when the budget cannot be met. Its central result comes from exact arithmetic on a fully specified bridge-inspection instance: machine-assisted supervised practice stretches time to competence from 1.2 to 4.05 years, so a city that sizes intake from certification hours provisions four trainee posts where fourteen are required.

We state the scope of the evidence plainly. The article reports no empirical or simulated result. Every number it prints is exact arithmetic on stated inputs, a value of the closed-form debt model, or an explicitly labeled target of an evaluation protocol that is specified and **has not been executed**. An earlier internal draft reported that protocol's targets as though the study had been run. A pre-submission audit identified the error, and that section has been removed in full. The worked allocation, its sensitivity frontier across six parameters, and two parameter shocks now carry the evidence. The Data Availability Statement, the public reference implementation (https://github.com/aliakarma/CivicWorkOS) and the manuscript describe this scope identically. The repository recomputes every checkable number in the article and reports the agreement check by check.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors have approved its submission. The authors declare no competing interests.
