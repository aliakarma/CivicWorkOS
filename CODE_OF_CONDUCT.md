# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences, especially on
  the normative and distributional questions this project's subject matter
  (automation, labour, and civic accountability) genuinely raises
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project
maintainers via the repository's issue tracker (for non-sensitive matters)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.
